# Code of conduct

We want Topiqu's issues, pull requests and community spaces to be welcoming to people of all
backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and feedback, credit others' work and accept responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy, personal boundaries and the time of contributors.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption
and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. Maintainers may
edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation or ban contributors according to the severity
and repetition of the conduct. Apply the same standards to maintainers and contributors.

For conduct concerns, contact a repository maintainer privately using a contact method listed on
their GitHub profile. Include relevant links and context without publishing private information.
If the concern involves a maintainer, contact another maintainer. Reports should be handled
confidentially to the extent possible while investigating and addressing the concern.
